— The honest part —

We aim for ~95%. One in twenty answers may be wrong.

Suits AI is an information and news tool, not a lawyer and not a substitute for one. Even leading legal-research AIs get a meaningful share of answers wrong, so we build for honesty over false confidence: every answer is cited, dated, and marked with how sure the system is. Treat it as an exceptionally well-read starting point — then verify anything important before you act. Nothing here creates an attorney–client relationship.
