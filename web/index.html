How it works

Retrieve the law. Cite the source. Show the doubt.

Tell it your situation

Describe your case — your visa type, your state, the notice you got. The more context, the sharper the answer.

It reads current law

Suits AI searches statutes, regulations, agency guidance, and case law — refreshed on a regular scanning schedule.

You get a cited answer

Plain-English explanation with links to the primary sources, a confidence signal, and an "accurate as of" date.

It flags its limits

When a question is high-stakes or uncertain, it says so plainly and tells you to consult a licensed attorney.

The honest part

We aim for ~95% — which means 1 in 20 answers may be wrong.

Suits AI is an information and news tool, not a lawyer, and not a substitute for one. Even leading legal-research AIs get a meaningful share of answers wrong, so we build for honesty over false confidence: every answer is cited, dated, and marked with how sure the system is. Treat it as an exceptionally well-read starting point — then verify anything important with the primary sources or a licensed attorney before you act. Nothing here creates an attorney–client relationship.
